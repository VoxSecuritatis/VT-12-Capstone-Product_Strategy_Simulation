ICP
- Primary: U.S. urban public-transit commuters (18–45) who ride buses/subways weekly, rely on personal audio to reduce transit noise, and shop sub-$100 headphones primarily on Amazon.
- Secondary: Students and budget-conscious office commuters seeking ANC value without premium pricing.

Value proposition
- Commute-tuned ANC and all-day comfort under $100—silence buses and trains without breaking your budget.

Messaging and channels
- Messaging pillars: 
  1) “Silence the commute, not your wallet” (effective ANC for buses/trains). 
  2) “Comfort and awareness for crowded rides” (low sound-leak + quick transparency). 
  3) “Reliable everyday value” (long battery, simple controls, solid warranty).
- Channel focus: 
  1) Amazon-first (optimized SEO/A+ listing, Sponsored Products/Brands, coupons, Vine). 
  2) Micro-influencer seeding on TikTok/YouTube (commuting/EDC/student creators) with affiliate links. 
  3) Paid social with commuter-context creative targeting urban ZIPs and commute hours.

Brief launch-plan outline
- Pre-launch (3–4 weeks): 
  - Validate commuter needs and size via quick rider intercept/survey and Amazon ad-copy tests; confirm under-$100 price and commuter feature set. 
  - Build Amazon listing (keywords like “noise cancelling headphones under $100,” “budget ANC”), A+ content, and comparison vs anchor competitor Soundcore Q20i. 
  - Queue Amazon Vine/early reviews; seed 20–30 micro-influencers with UGC briefs.
- Launch (Month 1): 
  - Amazon go-live with 10–15% coupon + Sponsored Products/Brands on core keywords; run commuter-context UGC ads (TikTok/Reels/Shorts). 
  - Secure reviews from budget-tech outlets; monitor ACOS/CTR/CVR daily; rapidly iterate main images/title bullets. 
  - Position head-to-head with Q20i on commuter-specific value (comfort, low leak, quick transparency).
- Post-launch (Month 2–3): 
  - Optimize keywords/creative; scale influencer affiliates; add Lightning Deals once ratings stabilize (≥4.3 with 50–100 reviews). 
  - Expand to Walmart.com once Amazon proof points are in; test geo-targeted transit-station ads.
- Success metrics: Amazon CVR ≥15%, ACOS ≤25%, 100+ reviews at ≥4.3 stars in 60 days, top-5 share of voice for “noise cancelling headphones under $100.”
