## Target customer/ICP

- US commuters 18-44 (core 25-34) riding bus/train/ride-share who juggle phone/laptop, want
  reliable calls, strong ANC, comfort, and quick charge under $100.
- Budget-savvy buyers comparing Soundcore/JBL on Amazon and big-box promos; value clear mics,
  dependable multipoint, compact fold, and a solid app/warranty.

## One-line value proposition

- Commuter-first ANC over-ears under $100 with clearer calls, rock-solid multipoint, all-day
  comfort/battery, fast USB-C charge, compact fold, and a better app/warranty.

## Messaging/channel suggestions

- "Hear less. Be heard more." Focus on call clarity + commuter ANC profiles; deploy via Amazon A+
  content and PDP video.
- "Switch devices without dropouts." Reliable multipoint demo via TikTok/IG Reels and seeded
  YouTube reviews.
- "15 minutes to 4 hours." Fast-charge + compact fold for daily rides; promote with retail shelf
  talkers and Amazon coupons.

## Launch plan (POC)

- **Pricing:** MSRP $99; launch promo $79 (guardrails $69-89 during deal windows). 2-year
  warranty, 30-day trial.
- **Readiness:** finalize commuter ANC presets (Bus/Train/Street), mic noise-reduction tuning, and
  app onboarding; QA for multipoint reliability.
- **Seeding:** 4 weeks pre-launch, place 50 units with transit/tech creators and budget audio
  reviewers; secure 15+ day-1 reviews on Amazon.
- **Amazon:** optimized PDP (keywords: "commuter ANC," "clear calls," "multipoint"), A+ comparison
  vs Q30/660NC, 7-day launch coupon + Limited-Time Deal.
- **Retail promo:** negotiate end-cap/feature at Target/Best Buy during commuter/back-to-school;
  include travel pouch bundle.
- **Measure/iterate:** track CVR, review velocity, return reasons, call-quality mentions; push
  week-2 firmware/app update if needed; flex promo only when JBL/Sony deal surges appear.
